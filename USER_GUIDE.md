# Quick Start Guide for Native Monitor

## What is this?

**Native Monitor** is like a health check-up for your computer. It tells you if your machine is happy, stressed, or needs a break - in plain English!

---

## 🚀 Getting Started

### Step 1: Open the Dashboard
1. Open your web browser
2. Go to: `http://localhost:5000`
3. That's it! You're in.

---

## 🎯 Understanding Your Dashboard

### The Big Emoji at the Top
This shows how your computer **feels** right now:

- 😌 **Calm** = Everything's great, computer is relaxed
- 🎯 **Focused** = Working hard but doing fine  
- 😰 **Stressed** = Getting overwhelmed, needs a break
- 🔥 **Beast Mode** = Maximum power! Close some apps soon

### The Score Circle
Think of this like a **health grade** (0-10):
- **8-10** = Excellent! Your computer is in top shape
- **6-7** = Good, but watch for warnings
- **4-5** = Fair, consider optimizing
- **0-3** = Poor, urgent action needed

---

## 📊 What the Numbers Mean

### CPU Power
**Translation:** How hard your computer's "brain" is working
- **0-50%** = Easy peasy ✅
- **50-75%** = Working hard ⚠️
- **75%+** = Struggling 🔥

### Memory
**Translation:** How much "desk space" your computer has left
- **0-60%** = Plenty of room ✅
- **60-80%** = Getting crowded ⚠️
- **80%+** = Almost full! 🔥

### Workload Limit
**Translation:** How much more you can safely do
- Shows how many more browser tabs you can open
- Or how many more apps you can run
- **Example:** "~15 tabs" = You can open 15 more tabs before things slow down

### Energy
**Translation:** How eco-friendly your computer is running
- **80-100** = Super efficient! 🌱
- **50-79** = Normal usage ⚡
- **0-49** = Power hungry 🔥

---

## 🤖 The AI Copilot

Think of this as your **computer's personal trainer**. It gives you tips like:
- "Close background apps for better speed"
- "Your system could use a restart"
- "You're running great today!"

---

## ⚡ Quick Actions (One-Click Fixes)

Click these buttons to instantly optimize:

### 🧹 Clear Memory
**What it does:** Frees up space by closing unused stuff
**When to use:** When Memory is above 70%

### ❄️ Cool Down CPU
**What it does:** Slows down background tasks so your main work is faster
**When to use:** When CPU is above 70%

### ⚡ Boost Mode
**What it does:** Makes everything run as fast as possible
**When to use:** Before gaming or video editing

### 🌱 Eco Mode
**What it does:** Saves battery and reduces heat
**When to use:** When on battery power

## 🎮 Keyboard Shortcuts

For power users:
- Press **R** = Refresh data
- Press **V** = Toggle detailed view
- Press **ESC** = Close pop-ups

---

## 🛡️ Privacy & Safety

### Is my data safe?
**YES!** Everything happens on your computer. Nothing is sent to the internet.

The banner says: *"Private & Secure: All analysis is performed locally. No data leaves your machine."*

## ❓ Common Questions

### Why is everything at 0%?
The app just started. Wait 3 seconds for data to load.

### What does "Balanced Mode" mean?
Your computer is a mix of work and play - not specialized for one thing.

### Can I use this while gaming?
Yes! It barely uses any resources itself.

### Does this slow down my computer?
No! It's super lightweight (under 50MB RAM).
